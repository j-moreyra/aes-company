# About Advanced Engineering Systems (AES)

**Web:** www.advengsys.com

## What we do

AES is the exclusive representative of Kukla in the Americas. Kukla is an Austrian
manufacturer with 90 years in weighing equipment. They engineer and build; we handle sales,
consultation, requirements gathering, quoting, design and delivery coordination, commissioning
assistance, and first-level support — escalating to Kukla engineers when needed.

## What we sell

High-accuracy, custom-engineered dynamic weighing equipment: **weigh feeders, belt scales,
loss-in-weight systems**. Every unit is engineered to order around the plant's material,
layout, and process. Nothing is off-the-shelf. Accuracy is ±1% or better.

The engagement is end-to-end: engineering and design → in-house production at Kukla → Factory
Acceptance Testing in Austria → DAP shipping to the plant → commissioning support →
recommended spare parts list → ongoing after-sales support with AES as standing point of
contact.

We do **not** perform installations. Clients use their own contractor or integrator.

## Revenue model

AES invoices the client directly with a markup on the Kukla equipment, then pays Kukla.
Project-based revenue — equipment sales plus spare parts. Not recurring, not subscription.
The CFO and CEO own AR, AP, and all financial transactions.

## Who we sell to

Manufacturing professionals responsible for bulk material handling in **gypsum, cement,
mining, and insulation** plants running 24/7. Four buying roles:

| Role | What they need |
|---|---|
| Plant Manager | Cost-effective, reliable systems; plant-wide efficiency |
| Maintenance Manager | Low-maintenance, long-lasting equipment; less downtime |
| Production Manager | Stable feed rates; consistent quality, minimal waste |
| Project Engineer | Clean integration; ROI justification |

**What they want:** consistently accurate feeding, less downtime and maintenance, less
material waste, clean integration into existing lines.

**What blocks them:** they don't trust their current readings, so operators manually adjust
feed rates. Budget limits and competing project priorities. Installs must land inside
scheduled maintenance windows. Most plants delay until the old system becomes impossible to
ignore.

**What they fear:** unplanned downtime costing thousands per hour, rising waste and
operational cost, buying the wrong system and being stuck with it.

## How a client starts

1. Share material, feed rate, and accuracy requirements.
2. Send plant layout or equipment diagram.
3. Review and approve the final custom design.

arrives on-site for commissioning and the client sourced the wrong cables themselves, the
weighing electronics can't talk to the weigh feeder. Commissioning day is lost.
