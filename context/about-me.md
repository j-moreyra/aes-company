## How I track work

No project management tool. Three surfaces:

- **HubSpot** — a deal per project, all contacts.
- **SharePoint via OneDrive** — all projects and documentation, synced to local disk with

- **Outlook flags** — my de-facto task list. Flagged mail is how I remember to reply.

If you're ever asked "what needs my attention," the answer lives in flagged Outlook mail and
open HubSpot deals, not in a task database.
