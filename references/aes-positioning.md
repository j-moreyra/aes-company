## The customer's problem, in their words

Plants keep outdated or inaccurate weighing systems not because they're satisfied, but because
of practical constraints:

- Operators don't trust the readings. The equipment technically functions, but inconsistency
  forces constant manual adjustment — more labor, more process instability, worse mix, worse
  product.
- Budget limits and competing project priorities. The payoff is long-term; the cost is upfront.
  Decision-makers wait until it fits the budget or until equipment failure makes it urgent.
- Installs must be planned around scheduled maintenance windows. Timing is its own constraint.
- Long-term benefits get underestimated until problems become severe.

**How it feels.** Frustration at constant recalibration. Pressure to justify rising maintenance
and material costs. Stress over hitting production targets on limited equipment. Firefighting
mode.

**How it spreads beyond work.** Late nights and weekend shifts clearing clogged equipment.
Physically demanding cleanouts. Chronic stress, fatigue, a reactive mindset. Lower morale and
turnover in critical roles.

**What they're afraid of.** Production halts costing thousands per hour. Rising operational
cost from waste. Investing in the wrong solution and being stuck with underperforming
equipment.

**What success looks like.** Consistently high-quality product from accurate feed rates. Lower
maintenance and downtime, freeing staff for other priorities. Reduced material waste and direct
cost savings.

## Why AES / Kukla

- **Engineered to order.** Not pre-set sizes or configurations. Modified intake chutes,
  integration into existing frames, adjusted discharge angles to prevent buildup or clogging.
  Critical where space is tight or material flow is unusual.
- **Built for abrasive environments.** High-grade, wear-resistant materials for cement, mining,
  gypsum, insulation. Lower maintenance frequency and cost over the equipment's life.
- **±1.0% accuracy or better** in most applications, from Kukla's proprietary weighing and
  control design. Stable feed rates, less variability, less overfeeding.
- **Consultative, hands-on support.** Direct access to the AES team from first conversation
  through commissioning and long-term support. No call centers, no being handed off.
- **90 years** of Kukla experience in the weighing space.

## Objections and how they're handled

**"What if it doesn't perform as expected / doesn't justify the cost?"**
Kukla's equipment is proven across industries. A formal performance guarantee has been issued
exactly once, because the equipment consistently delivers without needing one.

**"Can it handle our specific material?"**
For challenging products like fiberglass, the customer ships a sample to Kukla in Austria for
material testing before proceeding. Every fiberglass type tested so far has worked without
issue.

**"Kukla is in Austria — what about local support?"**
That's what AES is. Based in the Americas, first-line technical support, direct contact,
coordination with Kukla engineers, often a call set up same-day.

## Questions asked repeatedly

- **What's the accuracy?** ±1% or better, depending on application.
- **Do you handle installation?** No. Clients use their preferred contractor or local
  integrator.
- **Do you offer spare parts?** Yes — a recommended spare parts list ships with every purchase
  so clients can plan ahead and minimize downtime.

## Lesson learned

Communication cables can no longer be optional on a machine purchase. When a Kukla engineer
arrives for on-site commissioning and the client sourced the wrong cables independently, the
weighing electronics can't communicate with the weigh feeder — and the commissioning visit is
wasted.
